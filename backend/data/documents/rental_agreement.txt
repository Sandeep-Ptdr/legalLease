Educational sample knowledge-base document — not official law.
Useful rental clauses identify the parties and property, rent due date and method, allowed late charge, security deposit and return process, lease term and renewal, utilities and maintenance, visitor, pet, parking, alteration and subletting rules, notice and termination procedures, and a move-in inventory. When a lease expires, parties can agree to renew, move out, or continue periodically if contract and law allow. Notice periods depend on the contract, tenancy type and jurisdiction.
