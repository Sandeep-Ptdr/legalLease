A tenant should receive a clear written agreement and keep payment receipts and a signed copy. Tenants normally pay rent on time, keep the property reasonably clean, avoid damage beyond normal wear, and promptly report repair needs. A security deposit is money held to address unpaid rent or damage beyond normal wear and tear, subject to the agreement and applicable local law. Before moving in, both parties should record the property's condition with dated photographs and an inventory. Ask when and how the deposit is returned and what deductions can be made.
