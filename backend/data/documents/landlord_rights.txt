A landlord normally provides the property in the condition promised and should address maintenance affecting the agreed use of the home. The agreement should allocate routine upkeep, utilities and responsibility for tenant-caused damage. Whether rent can increase during a fixed lease depends on the agreement and applicable law. Ending a tenancy or seeking eviction generally needs notice and a lawful process; grounds, deadlines and procedures are jurisdiction-specific.
