IMPORTANT STATUS NOTE

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs states that States/UTs must adopt it through their own legislation or amendment. I did not verify a Gujarat enactment adopting the Model Tenancy Act, 2021. Therefore, the Model Tenancy Act should not be treated as Gujarat law. The Gujarat Rents, Hotel and Lodging House Rates Control Act, 1947 historically governed rent control, repairs and eviction in covered premises. Because the current post-31 March 2026 status and application to a particular property must be checked against the latest Gujarat legislation/Gazette, the 1947 Act should not automatically be treated as governing every residential tenancy in Gujarat in 2026. LANDLORD ACCESS, INSPECTION AND NOTICE

For an ordinary lease governed by the Transfer of Property Act, 1882, the Act does not provide a general Gujarat-specific statutory right allowing a landlord unrestricted entry into a rented residential property. Entry rights can depend on the tenancy agreement and the applicable local tenancy law.

The Model Tenancy Act, 2021 contains specific provisions concerning landlord/tenant rights, including entry for inspection and repairs, but it is only a model law unless adopted by the relevant State/UT. It should therefore not be used as a Gujarat-law rule without verifying adoption.

For Gujarat: Not verified; check the current local law and the tenancy agreement before stating a specific statutory notice period for landlord inspection or entry.

MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

The Gujarat Rents, Hotel and Lodging House Rates Control Act, 1947 contains Section 23, titled "Landlord's duty to keep premises in good repair." This provision is part of the historical Gujarat rent-control framework. Its applicability to a particular property in Gujarat in 2026 must be separately verified because the Act contains exemptions and its statutory duration has changed through amendments.

Source: [Gujarat Rents, Hotel and Lodging House Rates Control Act, 1947 — India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/4655/1/rentshotelact.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com). Section 23. Date checked: 8 October 2026.

The Transfer of Property Act, 1882 defines a lease and rent in Section 105. Section 108 sets out statutory rights and liabilities of lessors and lessees, subject to the contract or local law.

ENDING A TENANCY AND EVICTION

Under Section 106 of the Transfer of Property Act, 1882, where there is no contract, local law or usage to the contrary, a lease for a purpose other than agriculture or manufacturing is generally treated as month-to-month and may be terminated by fifteen days' written notice expiring with the end of the tenancy month. The section also prescribes requirements concerning the form and delivery of notice.

This rule is expressly subject to a contrary contract, local law or usage. It therefore should not be presented as an unconditional eviction rule for every Gujarat tenancy.

Source: [Transfer of Property Act, 1882 — India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/14037/1/transfer_of_property_act8_%281%29.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com). Section 106. Date checked: 8 October 2026.

A lease from year to year, for a term exceeding one year, or reserving yearly rent generally requires a registered instrument under Section 107 of the Transfer of Property Act, 1882.

Source: [Transfer of Property Act, 1882 — India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/14037/1/transfer_of_property_act8_%281%29.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com). Section 107. Date checked: 8 October 2026.

The Registration Act, 1908 also identifies leases from year to year, leases for terms exceeding one year, and leases reserving yearly rent as documents requiring registration, subject to the statutory provisions and exceptions.

Source: [Registration Act, 1908 — India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/14646/1/ra1908-16.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com). Section 17(1)(d). Date checked: 8 October 2026.

A landlord should not be described as having an automatic right to physically evict a tenant without following the applicable legal process. The exact forum, grounds, notice requirements and procedure depend on the applicable tenancy law, agreement, property and facts.

For a current Gujarat-specific eviction procedure applicable to all residential premises: Not verified; check the current local law and competent court/authority.

MODEL-LAW STATUS

The Model Tenancy Act, 2021 is not a central tenancy statute automatically applicable throughout India. MoHUA states that it was circulated to States and UTs for adoption through fresh legislation or amendment of existing rental laws.

Source: [MoHUA — Model Tenancy Act policy information](https://mohua.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/dec-2023-english.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com). Date checked: 8 October 2026.

For Gujarat, no verified primary-source evidence was located establishing that the Model Tenancy Act, 2021 itself is currently in force as Gujarat law. Therefore: Do not state that the Model Tenancy Act applies in Gujarat without verifying the latest Gujarat legislation and Gazette notification.
