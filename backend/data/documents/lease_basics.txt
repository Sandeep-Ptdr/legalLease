Educational sample knowledge-base document — not official law.
A rental agreement or lease is a contract between a property owner or authorized landlord and a tenant. It should identify the home, parties, rent, payment date, security deposit, duration, utilities, maintenance expectations, renewal, termination and notice requirements. Before signing, read every clause and fill blank spaces. Confirm occupants, pets, parking, subletting, repairs and a move-in condition inventory. A fixed-term lease runs for its stated period; a periodic tenancy continues until ended with required notice.
